Third-Party Content
-------------------

This dataset contains synthetic product descriptions generated with
large language models. All product identifiers, brand names, model
identifiers, and other potentially identifying signals have been
anonymised during generation and further scrubbed in a second-pass
sweep at release time. No customer data, seller data, or personally
identifying information is included.
